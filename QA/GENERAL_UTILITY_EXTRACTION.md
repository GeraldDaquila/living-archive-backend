# General Utility Extraction — v487.79 candidate

## Purpose

Recover the reusable general visitor capability buried in the v461–v482 USE lineage without
resurrecting topic-specific calibration code.

The candidate is deliberately isolated. It is not wired into production in v487.79.

## Architectural position

USE/The Guide remains the macro conductor. A specialist owns its narrow domain and voice.
Canonical assets remain authoritative. General Utility is a bounded fallback instrument available
only after USE authorizes it.

## Recovered practices

The candidate preserves the strongest broadly reusable patterns found in the historical lineage:

- inquiry-shape classification
- supplied-evidence cleaning and deduplication
- sentence/candidate ranking
- claim normalization
- bounded factual/conceptual construction
- bounded lived-experience orientation
- canonical-doorway candidates rather than canonical authority
- explicit unavailable/retry/delegate states

Topic-specific family catalogs and historical special cases are intentionally excluded.

## Not yet standardized

Provider arbitration, final visitor-language realization, and cross-tool response envelopes are
not yet promoted here. Those should be standardized only after comparison against HRN and other
live specialist behavior.

## Promotion gate

Before wiring this into USE, verify:

1. the module is isolated and importable;
2. no production path imports it;
3. tests reject fabrication when evidence is absent;
4. safety-sensitive requests delegate rather than answer;
5. the utility cannot claim routing or canonical authority;
6. specialist voice cannot be overwritten by this module;
7. a future integration can be placed behind one explicit USE authorization seam.
