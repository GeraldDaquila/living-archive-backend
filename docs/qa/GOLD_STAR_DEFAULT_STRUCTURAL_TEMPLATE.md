# Default Gold Star Structural Template — The Guide

**Status:** Governing default for visitor-facing Guide responses  
**Effective:** 2026-11-18

## Default architecture

The Gold Star standard is the default structural architecture across the Guide, not merely an optional quality floor for the general-purpose composer. It applies to ordinary answers, specialist routes, retrieval-grounded composition, deterministic recovery, provider-exhaustion fallback, and final response repair.

1. **Answer or perform the actual task directly.** Begin with the substantive answer, not a description of the question or a process preface.
2. **Develop what makes the answer useful.** Supply the reasoning, evidence, mechanism, comparison, context, or usable method the task requires.
3. **Complete the movement.** Add a distinct implication, limit, practical test, consequence, or next movement that follows from what was established.
4. **Keep navigation separate.** Add a verified, materially relevant Archive doorway after the answer. A doorway never substitutes for the answer.

For substantive explanatory or conceptual questions, the default presentation is three connected, readable paragraphs, each performing a distinct cognitive operation. A bounded factual lookup, explicit concise request, or task that genuinely requires a different deliverable may use a shorter answer, table, checklist, or steps, but the alternative must meet or exceed the same standards of directness, completeness, grounding, care, calibration, and usefulness.

## Subject adaptation

The template is structural, not a universal subject-matter template. Before drafting, identify the visitor's actual cognitive task and subject. Adapt the reasoning sequence, vocabulary, examples, and paragraph functions to that task. A comparison may need parallel treatment; a procedure needs usable steps; an ambiguity question needs contextual disambiguation; an evidentiary question needs source and claim boundaries. These adaptations change what the structural moves contain; they do not make the default architecture optional.

Specialized Gold Star lessons contribute only their approved reasoning discipline when the question genuinely qualifies. They never transfer an exemplar's answer, factual claims, examples, citations, or doorway. Broad keyword overlap is insufficient.

## Gold Star learning governance

A recurring pattern across different visitor questions is a candidate signal, not proof. The candidate lesson must be grounded in authorized, privacy-preserving evidence; identify a shared missing reasoning operation rather than a shared topic; state a corrective rule and qualifying conditions; define an abstention/transfer boundary; and pass held-out target, adjacent-task, and unrelated negative-control evaluation. Human review is required before approval or activation. When cross-visitor evidence is unavailable, the system may explain the method but must not claim to have detected a real pattern.

## Release and verification

The structural template must be present in the live compact composition prompt and protected by regression tests. The final visitor boundary must continue to enforce the applicable task-specific response contract after specialist, fallback, and integrity-repair paths. Exact Gold Star answers remain separately frozen and immutable; adding a new benchmark does not authorize rewriting existing records or allocating a display serial.
