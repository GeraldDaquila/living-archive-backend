# Gold Star Transferability Review Matrix

**Status:** Governance review — not a production activation document  
**Purpose:** Distinguish an excellent answer from an explicitly approved, reusable structural lesson.

## Governing rule

A Gold Star may be an exact-query benchmark without being eligible for cross-question learning. Cross-question use requires explicit human approval of the reasoning discipline, a defined qualifying question family, explicit abstention boundaries, and a prohibition on transferring answer text, facts, examples, citations, or doorway destinations.

Approval of this matrix alone does not change runtime behavior. A separate, reviewable registry change is required before any candidate is marked runtime_transfer true.

## Candidate A — Complexity and uncertainty

**Registry ID:** general-guide-complexity-and-uncertainty-gold-standard-2026-10-26

**Transferable structural principle:** Distinguish concepts before connecting them. Explain interaction without treating related concepts as identical, and preserve calibrated uncertainty.

**Qualifying question family:** Questions asking how two or more related concepts differ, interact, or should be distinguished.

**Required abstention:** Do not match on shared vocabulary alone. Abstain when the visitor is asking for a factual definition, a canonical Living Archive term, or a relationship outside the candidate's conceptual pattern.

**Never transfer:** The approved wording, examples, factual claims, citations, or continuation doorway.

**Human approval:** ☐ Approved for structural transfer  ☐ Keep exact-only

## Candidate B — Fragmented knowledge and wise action

**Registry ID:** general-guide-fragmented-knowledge-wise-action-gold-standard-2026-10-26

**Transferable structural principle:** Reconnect relevant facts, experience, and perspectives; distinguish what is known, inferred, and missing; keep decisions open to correction without forcing consensus.

**Qualifying question family:** Questions about fragmented information, siloed knowledge, disconnected perspectives, or acting wisely when relevant understanding is distributed.

**Required abstention:** Abstain when integration is not the visitor's problem, when a specific factual answer is required, or when the question concerns a canonical term whose source material should control the response.

**Never transfer:** The cross-team example, approved wording, factual claims, citations, or continuation doorway.

**Human approval:** ☐ Approved for structural transfer  ☐ Keep exact-only

## Candidate C — Conflicting information and discernment

**Registry ID:** general-guide-conflicting-information-discernment-gold-star-2026-11-07

**Transferable structural principle:** Separate verified information from inference, interpretation, assumption, and unresolved claims; examine source independence; calibrate action to stakes and reversibility while preserving correctability.

**Qualifying question family:** Questions about conflicting information, competing claims, uncertainty about what is sufficiently reliable to act upon, or recovering orientation amid informational overload.

**Required abstention:** Abstain from generic anxiety or productivity questions unless the core difficulty is genuinely epistemic. Do not treat emotional distress alone as evidence of this pattern.

**Never transfer:** The approved response wording, emotional framing, examples, factual claims, citations, or continuation doorway.

**Human approval:** ☐ Approved for structural transfer  ☐ Keep exact-only

## Activation protocol

No candidate enters live cross-question learning until:

1. a human reviewer explicitly approves the candidate;
2. the registry records runtime_transfer true for that candidate only;
3. qualifying variants and abstention cases are added;
4. held-out evaluation passes; and
5. any promotion from shadow observation into composition is separately reviewed.

**Default:** When uncertain, abstain.
