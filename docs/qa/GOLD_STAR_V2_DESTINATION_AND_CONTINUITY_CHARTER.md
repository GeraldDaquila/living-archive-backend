## 3. Final destination

Gold Star v2 is a source-independent, question-adaptive first-response quality system. Every completed visitor answer—whether generated by an LLM, a deterministic handler, a specialist, a recovery path, or a future supported source—must be produced and assessed through one coherent quality lifecycle.

The target lifecycle is:

1. Understand the visitor's actual task and constraints.
2. Establish a structured, question-specific contract.
3. Select a capable answer route and its evidence requirements.
4. Compose or execute the task in the appropriate form.
5. Validate objective integrity, evidence, task adequacy, and applicable quality criteria.
6. Perform bounded, evidence-directed repair when safe and useful.
7. Deliver a complete answer, or honestly clarify/abstain/disclose the limitation.
8. Add a separate canonical doorway only when verified, relevant, useful, and permitted.
9. Learn only through governed evidence, transfer boundaries, evaluation, and explicit approval.

This is one system with shared contracts and lifecycle stages—not one generic composer forced onto every question. Specialist knowledge and purpose-built deterministic capability remain legitimate. They must, however, declare their capabilities and participate in the shared contract and final validation boundaries.

## 4. Integrated architecture

### A. Question understanding and contract

A versioned, structured question contract is the common interface between all answer routes and the quality lifecycle. It must represent, where applicable:

- primary and secondary tasks, including multi-intent and nested requests;
- domain/subdomain and audience;
- stakes and consequence of error;
- requested output, decision, or deliverable;
- explicit constraints and prohibited actions;
- reasoning mode and task-specific completion criteria;
- evidence and provenance requirements;
- material uncertainty and unresolved assumptions;
- response shape;
- clarification, abstention, and handoff requirements.

Unknown, mixed-intent, and ambiguous states are valid. Surface phrase matching may provide candidate signals or detect explicit hard constraints; it must not claim semantic certification. Explicit user constraints take precedence over weak inferred preferences.

### B. Capability-aware route selection

Each supported route declares what it can reliably do, what evidence it can use, and what it must decline or hand off.

- LLM composition may handle broad synthesis where supported, subject to grounding and validation.
- Exact approved Gold Star answers retain exact-query authority only.
- Purpose-built deterministic handlers may answer within their tested domain and declared capability.
- Generic deterministic recovery may not simulate subject competence or fabricate completion.
- Specialist routes retain their domain-specific protocols and structured handoff/error contracts.
- Provider-exhaustion and exception recovery must be honest about reduced capability.

Route choice is not a quality judgment. Passing a shared response-envelope check does not establish equivalent reasoning, factuality, completeness, or utility.

### C. Shared answer envelope and provenance

All completed visitor answers use a common structured envelope. At minimum it carries:

- request/query identity or privacy-safe correlation reference;
- answer payload and completion state;
- source/route class and provenance;
- question-contract version and applicable task contract;
- evidence references where relevant;
- validation outcomes and any repair outcome;
- separate optional doorway/recommendation metadata.

Structured handoffs and explicit error payloads retain their own schemas and are not mislabeled as completed answers.

Do not log visitor query or answer text merely to prove the system is active. Use privacy-safe diagnostics and test fixtures.

### D. Layered quality and validation lifecycle

Validation is deliberately layered; there is no single semantic score that can certify everything.

1. **Hard objective invariants:** schema/transport integrity, required deliverable presence, explicit constraint adherence, unsafe output, invalid/unverified links, exact-answer immutability, and other defects with reliable machine evidence.
2. **Evidence validation:** claim support, source identity/relevance, temporal validity, calculations, code/configuration verification, attribution, and uncertainty.
3. **Question-specific adequacy:** whether the requested task was actually performed and its completion criteria met.
4. **Human quality judgment:** prose, structure, coherence, discernment, insight, proportionality, and practical utility where automated evidence is insufficient.

Heuristics may be diagnostic signals. They must not certify meaning or hard-fail a response without calibrated reliability. A response-floor gate that checks presence, size, markup, and generic non-answer markers is an integrity safeguard—not proof of Gold Star intellectual quality.

### E. Bounded repair and honest resolution

For non-frozen answers only:

1. Identify the exact failed criterion and supporting evidence.
2. Repair the smallest deficient part while preserving valid content and constraints.
3. Re-run relevant validators.
4. Stop after a bounded number of attempts and within explicit cost/time limits.
5. If still deficient, ask one necessary clarification, abstain, or disclose the limitation.

No infinite retries, silent degradation, or uncontrolled provider/Pinecone spend. Frozen Gold Star answers are never silently rewritten; a failed frozen record is withheld for governed review.

### F. Answer and doorway are separate

The answer must meet the task before navigation is considered. A doorway is optional and must be canonical, verified, materially relevant, useful, and not prohibited by the visitor. A title or keyword match alone is insufficient. An intentional absence of a doorway is valid when no coherent destination exists.

### G. Governed learning and Gold Star promotion

The eight Gold Star dimensions remain universal evaluation criteria, not eight mandatory visible sections.

A candidate learning pattern is not an approved lesson. Cross-question transfer requires evidence, a shared reasoning operation, qualifying context, explicit abstention boundary, held-out positive and negative-control tests, human review, provenance, and explicit approval.

Never transfer exemplar answer text, facts, examples, citations, or doorways into unrelated questions. Never modify existing approved Gold Star records, benchmark identities, exact text, provenance, numbering, or freeze policies without separate record-specific authorization.

Do not implement the project as a series of disconnected patches. Build and test these integrated work packages in dependency order.

### WP1 — Unified question-to-answer contract (integrated foundation)

Implement together:

- capability declarations for each answer route;
- common answer envelope and provenance;
- shared lifecycle orchestration and consistent final boundary;
- separate answer/doorway handling;
- privacy-safe diagnostics and contract version visibility.

Do not treat the initial classifier alone as completion. The contract must be consumed by every supported path in the correct way, without violating specialist integrity boundaries or flattening specialist expertise.

**Acceptance:** Contract-consumption tests prove the same governed task information reaches every supported route/boundary; route-specific capabilities and abstention are respected; unknown/mixed intent stays valid; no source can bypass final objective integrity checks.

### WP2 — Evidence-aware validation and bounded resolution (one package)

Implement together:
- objective invariant validators;
- evidence/provenance checks appropriate to task and claim type;
- question-specific completion checks;
- calibrated non-blocking semantic review signals where reliable hard enforcement is not possible;
- bounded repair, revalidation, clarification, abstention, and honest failure behavior;
- immutable exact-answer protection.

**Acceptance:** Positive, negative, adversarial, and failure-mode tests demonstrate that valid answers pass, objective defects are caught, unsupported claims are not certified, repairs are bounded, frozen answers remain unchanged, and incapable routes do not fabricate answers.

### WP3 — Representative evaluation and human calibration (one package)

Implement together:
- real paired LLM/deterministic captures only where both routes genuinely support the task;
- representative held-out question/domain/stakes coverage;
- provenance and exact runtime/commit identity for every capture;
- blinded human review against the eight dimensions;
- evaluator calibration against human judgments, including false-positive/false-negative reporting;
- explicit unsupported-route and missing-capture reporting.

**Acceptance:** No fabricated fixtures; no parity claims from missing outputs; no automated quality certification without calibration; reviewers can reproduce which code and contract produced each answer.

### WP4 — Governed learning and question-specific elevation (one package)

Implement together:
- evidence-backed recurring-pattern detection;
- reusable structural correction before question-specific refinement;
- qualifying context and abstention boundary;
- target, adjacent, and unrelated negative-control tests;
- explicit human/user approval;
- registry/provenance/freeze/numbering preservation.

**Acceptance:** A new lesson cannot become active or a Gold Star without all governance gates; unrelated questions remain unaffected; approved records remain immutable.

### WP5 — Controlled release and live acceptance

Implement release preparation as one controlled operation:
- complete CI and regression suite on exact candidate commit;
- registry and transfer-review reconciliation;
- human review evidence;
- deterministic capability honesty;
- primary and secondary browser acceptance using actual visitor interaction;
- controlled deployment order and post-deploy health/commit verification;
- bounded monitoring and rollback criteria.

**Acceptance:** Exact candidate identity is proven on both services; browser behavior is observed; no production deployment is inferred from CI alone. Never deploy merely to make a test possible if a safe test environment can answer the question. Do not create extra Render services or trigger duplicate deployments.

## 6. Test strategy

Every work package must include tests at four levels:

1. **Contract/unit:** schema, version, invariants, explicit constraints, capability declarations.
2. **Route integration:** each supported route consumes and preserves the contract/envelope correctly.
3. **Cross-route and negative controls:** same question across supported routes, mixed/unknown intent, unsupported tasks, no-handoff, absent evidence, doorway omission, frozen answer, and unrelated questions.
4. **Visitor acceptance:** actual browser interaction after automated gates pass, first on the approved test target and then primary/secondary release acceptance.

Tests must exercise behavior, not merely check that a file, prompt fragment, selector, log line, or class exists. Test fixtures must be attributable and never presented as real captures.
