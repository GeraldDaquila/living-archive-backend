# RCA Profile Synthesis Library v1.1

## Status
Canonical interpretation asset, draft for simulation and editorial approval. It is separate from the Woody user-interface snippet.

## Governing purpose
The assessment must do more than report four scores. It should help a person or group understand:
1. what the profile suggests about how it stays in touch with what is happening;
2. why the pattern matters in everyday decisions and consequences;
3. where productive further exploration can begin.

## Deterministic synthesis
The report is assembled from the four dimension scores, their public capacity bands, their relative gaps, the dominant profile shape, and the selected perspective (Individual or Collective). The Individual and Collective narratives are independently authored; Collective text is never created merely by replacing pronouns.

A meaningful synthesis must name the actual dimensions and scores involved, explain the relationship between them, explain the practical consequence, and offer an appreciative inquiry grounded in an existing strength or example of the capacity working.

## Pattern coverage
The library provides distinct Individual and Collective narratives for six relational patterns:
- R_P: Resonance exceeds Correspondence — noticing signals vs. making sense of them.
- C_P: Coherence exceeds Correspondence — internal agreement vs. checking the shared picture.
- P_K: Correspondence exceeds Correctability — recognizing a problem vs. changing course.
- K_C: Correctability exceeds Coherence — adjusting vs. maintaining shared direction.
- R_C: Resonance exceeds Coherence — noticing signals vs. integrating them with priorities.
- P_R: Correspondence exceeds Resonance — careful reasoning vs. incomplete incoming information.

Four profile shapes provide fallback context: High Balanced, Balanced, Low Balanced, and Uneven.

## Composition contract
Three paragraphs:
1. dominant profile story in ordinary vocabulary;
2. why that pattern matters in lived or organizational consequences;
3. developmental doorway, starting from what is already working.

The overall average is shown as a secondary indicator only. It cannot override the four-dimensional profile.

## Scope and limits
Patterns identify useful questions; they do not establish causes. The instrument is non-diagnostic, developmental, secular, and organizationally applicable. The sequence remains Resonance → Coherence → Correspondence → Correctability → renewed Resonance.

## Versioning
Changes to interpretive meaning are made in this library first. Woody is only the presentation and interaction layer. This v1.1 library is not yet wired into the live snippet; connect only after simulation and QA establish correct precedence, coverage, and language quality.
